Why cold brew

Smoother coffee, by design

Hot coffee is acidic and harsh — the last thing you want sloshing around before a hard effort. Cold brew extraction steeps our beans for 16 hours in cold water, pulling out flavour and caffeine while leaving most of the acid behind.

The result is up to 67% less acid than hot coffee — a smoother, naturally sweet cup that sits light whether you drink it before a run or after one.

  • Cold water only, never heated
  • Single-origin beans from Cerrado, Brazil
  • USDA Organic — nothing added

Frequently asked questions

Good to know
How much caffeine is in each bottle? +
Classic is about 120mg — roughly a strong cup of coffee. Race steps up to ~240mg with organic green-coffee extract for hard efforts. Recovery is a lighter ~95mg so it won't keep you up after an evening run.
Why cold brew instead of regular coffee? +
Cold brewing steeps the grounds in cold water for 16 hours, which extracts far less acid than hot brewing — up to 67% less. That makes it smoother, naturally sweeter, and much easier on your stomach before and after running.
When should I drink each one? +
Classic for daily and easy miles, 30–45 minutes before you head out. Race about 45 minutes before a hard session or start line. Recovery within the hour after you finish, to rehydrate and refuel.
Is it really organic? +
Yes — USDA Organic certified, made with natural-process beans from a single farm in Cerrado, Brazil. No sugar, syrup, milk, or artificial anything.
How should I store it, and how long does it keep? +
Keep it refrigerated and enjoy cold. Sealed, it stays fresh for months; once opened, drink within 3 days for the best flavour.
Is it safe for competition? +
2nd Wind contains no banned substances — just organic coffee, water, and (in Recovery) basic electrolytes. Always check current rules for your federation if you compete at the elite level.
