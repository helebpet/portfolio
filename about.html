About Me

Hello! I'm Petra Helebrantová, a Czech multidisciplinary digital designer. During the academic year you will find me in San Francisco, and over the summer in Prague. Most of my work sits in branding and UX/UI, but once in a while I like to step away from my specialization and just create some art.

I'm a senior at the University of San Francisco, majoring in design with a minor in UX/UI↗. On campus, I work as a Technology Design Tutor, helping other students through coding and software problems, and as a graphic designer at Ampersand Design Studio, run by one of my professors.

Most of what drives my work comes from time away from the screen of my laptop. I run and spend as much time outdoors as I can. I also almost always carry a camera with me to capture ordinarily extraordinary moments that catch my eye.

As a designer, my goal is to empower ethical and environmentally conscious businesses in health, wellness, and sport through thoughtful design. For me, great design should be sustainable, distinctive, intuitive, and carefully considered. I'm always excited to collaborate with brands and organizations working toward meaningful change and deeper human connections.
