Concept

2nd Wind is a brand of organic cold brew coffee, made for runners who chase morning miles before the rest of the city is awake. The audience, "The Early Miler," logs splits on Strava, joins weekend run clubs, and is drawn to performance-led product design, minimal aesthetics, and the small rituals that bookend a training session. 2nd Wind is positioned as the only cold brew built around running itself: low acid, high caffeine, brewed overnight, sized for one bottle before or after a run. The tone of voice sits at the intersection of three attributes: bold, honest, and quietly utilitarian.

Brand Identity

The identity is built around a "Running Track" idea: every mark in the system traces back to the oval geometry of a 400-metre track, from the stacked lanes of the symbol to the rounded letterforms of the wordmark. The wordmark uses the numeral "2" instead of spelling out "second" so the mark stays compact and distinct, and a separate concentric-oval symbol sits like a top-down view of a stadium for moments where the full wordmark won't fit. The brand voice lands on "Brewed for Runners," carried by three product pillars: Single Origin, Cold Brewed, Organic.

Typography pairs Mono45 Headline, a condensed monospaced display face, with B612 Mono Regular, originally drawn for in-flight displays. The palette is grounded and athletic: White and Off White set the page, Black anchors the brand, and three accents (Trail #818370 olive, Ice Bath #758D8F dusty teal, and Golden Hour #C09645 mustard) map directly onto the three product variants. Two custom round badges, "Single Origin Brazil" and a Berkeley Based stamp, echo the concentric-ring construction of the secondary symbol.

Applications

Three bottle variants share a single structural label: matte glass, screen-printed graphics, foil cap. Classic, Race, and Recovery each get their own accent colour and ingredient story, so the line reads as a family without losing each variant's identity. A grainy, high-contrast photography style sits alongside, leaning into motion blur, golden hour light, and real training settings.

The system stretches into apparel (t-shirt, tote, cap), takeaway coffee cups, the "Run. Recover. Repeat." training journal, an Instagram-led social presence, and a black-led website where the wordmark anchors the homepage and the track symbol carries the navigation.
