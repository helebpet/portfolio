CV

Petra Helebrantová

I'm a Czech multidisciplinary digital designer working mostly in branding and UX/UI. During the academic year you will find me in San Francisco, and over the summer in Prague. I'm a senior at the University of San Francisco, majoring in design with a minor in UX/UI, and I draw creative inspiration from both European and American design cultures.

I move comfortably between design and code, building interactive, hand-coded websites and working fluently across Adobe Creative Suite and Figma for UX/UI and branding work. Most of what drives my work comes from time away from the screen, from running and time outdoors to the camera I almost always carry with me. My goal is to empower ethical and environmentally conscious businesses in health, wellness, and sport through thoughtful design.
