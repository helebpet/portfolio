Petra Helebrantová

I'm a Czech multidisciplinary digital designer with a special interest in UX/UI design and branding, based in San Francisco and Prague. I'm studying design with a minor in UX/UI at the University of San Francisco, heading into my senior year in fall 2026, and I draw creative inspiration from both European and American design cultures.

I move comfortably between design and code, building interactive, hand-coded websites and working fluently across Adobe Creative Suite and Figma for UX/UI and branding work. I'm especially drawn to projects focused on sustainability, environmental responsibility, health, wellness, and sport. For me, great design should be sustainable, distinctive, intuitive, and carefully considered.
